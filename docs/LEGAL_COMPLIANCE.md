# Beniffy — Indian Regulatory & Legal Compliance Documentation (Item 2.10)

## 1. Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Payment & Closed-Loop Instrument Compliance
- **Points Classification**: Loyalty points are issued solely as non-transferable, non-cashable promotional credits earned from completed telecom/retail purchases. Paid fiat currency never converts directly into points.
- **Closed-Loop Operator Ecosystem**: Points are redeemable exclusively for Beniffy platform ISP services (Broadband, Cable TV) and cannot be transferred between users or withdrawn to bank accounts.
- **Payment Processing**: Customer funds are routed directly via licensed payment gateways (Razorpay). No customer money is held in un-segregated platform accounts.

## 2. Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) & DND Regulations
- **Transactional Communications**: OTPs, order confirmation, and delivery updates are classified as Transactional SMS and transmitted strictly under registered DND-exempt headers.
- **Promotional Marketing**: Promotional messages are restricted by user opt-in consent (`sms_marketing`, `email_marketing`) and checked against the National Do Not Call (DND) Registry before dispatch.

## 3. Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act Compliance
- **Data Minimization**: Beniffy collects only essential parameters (phone number, name, delivery address) strictly required to fulfill retail delivery and ISP service binding.
- **Explicit Consent**: User registration requires explicit checkbox confirmation agreeing to Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
- **User Privacy Rights**: Right to access, update, or request deletion of personal records via `privacy@beniffy.com`.

## 4. Income Tax & GST Compliance
- **Data Retention**: Pursuant to Indian Income Tax and Goods and Services Tax (GST) regulations, order transaction records and invoices are immutably archived for a mandatory 7-year statutory period.
